# GAA Match Planner – Help

**Remember: no plan survives first contact**
Use this tool to trial, visualize and share plans with fellow coaches. 

It is no substitute for reacting to the game that actually plays out. 
**Trust it alone and it might let you down!**

Use it to trial offensive and defensive selections and increase your confidence around what you are trying to achieve in a game. 

A planner for underage hurling and gaelic football mentors. You build your panel once, tick who's here on match day, get a fair starting selection with one tap, and then adjust it by hand.

Everything is saved on your own phone. Nothing is uploaded unless you send a link or a picture.

## The process

1. **Panel tab:** define your panel. For each player choose where they usually play and whether they're a left- or right-sided player.
2. **Here today tab:** set up the match. Tick who's here, set the half length and change times, and mark rotation players (**B**) and players who stay in one position (**🔒**).
3. **Plan tab:** press the **🪄 wand** for a starting selection across the whole match.
4. **Tweak by hand:** swap players to get the team you want. Use **↶ undo** and **↷ redo** if you change your mind.
5. **Share:** send the pitch as a picture, or send your plan to another mentor's phone.
6. **Save** versions of your plan (📁 Plans) to compare them or come back to them later.

---

## 1. Panel

- The list shows your whole panel. Tap **✎** to change a player's name, positions or side, or to remove them.
- **+ Add player(s)** opens three ways to add players:
  - Type one player's name and tap their positions (GK, FB, HB, MID, HF, FF) and side.
  - **Import a ClubZap RSVP report (.csv).** Anyone not already on the panel is added. Nobody is removed.
  - Paste a list, one player per line, such as `Seán Murphy, HF MID, L`.
- Players marked **no position** still play, but the wand can't place them well. Set their positions first.

## 2. Here today

- **Opposition and date** appear on the plan and on the picture.
- **Half length** and **change times**: tap **Quarters** for changes at each quarter. You can also add your own times.
- **Subs allowed** is **No limit** by default. Choose **Set a limit** if your competition caps substitutions.
- **Who's here:** tick players as they arrive. **Load ClubZap RSVP report** the night before ticks everyone who said they're attending and marks the rest "no reply". Adjust it on the day.
- For each player who's here:
  - **B**: a rotation player. B players share the bench time, and everyone not marked B plays the whole match.
    - Example: 14 here for 13 places with changes each quarter, so one player sits out each quarter. Mark 4 players B and each sits out one quarter (37½ minutes each). Mark 3 and one of them sits out twice. Mark 2 and they get half a match each.
    - Include your sub(s) when you mark B. An unmarked sub plays the whole match, and a B player starts on the bench instead.
    - If you mark no more B players than there are bench places, B players start on the bench and everyone rotates, so nobody sits out all match. The page tells you when this happens.
    - Mark nobody and everyone rotates.
    - A line under the instructions shows how many B players are sharing how many bench places.
    - When a B player comes on, they take the position of the B player going off. Choose B players who can cover each other's positions, or lock (🔒) the ones who must stay put.
  - **🔒**: stays in one position for the whole match. When they come back on, they go back to the same spot.
  - **⏱**: arrives late, or went off injured at a given minute.
- **Start a new match** clears the ticks, marks and plan. It keeps your panel and settings.

## 3. Plan

- **Team size and formation** are at the top. Formation is written GK-FB-HB-MID-HF-FF, for example `1-2-3-2-3-2`.
- **Q1 / Q2 / Q3 / Q4** show who's where in each quarter. The bench for that quarter is at the bottom of the pitch.
- **🪄 wand** (top left of the pitch) fills in the whole match automatically:
  - It evens out minutes.
  - It keeps 🔒 players in their positions.
  - It rotates only the **B** players when you've marked more B players than there are bench places. Everyone else plays the whole match.
  - It works around late arrivals and injuries.
  - Press it again for a different selection. ↶ takes you back.
  - Nothing moves automatically until you press the wand.
- **Swapping players:** tap a player, then another, or press and hold one and drag it. A swap applies from the quarter you're looking at to the end of the match.
  - Two players on the pitch swap **positions only**. Who sits out each quarter doesn't change.
  - A bench player swapped with a pitch player takes over the rest of that player's match, and vice versa.
- **↶ / ↷** (top right of the pitch) undo and redo your last actions. They're greyed out when there's nothing to undo or redo. The history lasts until you close the page.
- **Warnings** (yellow) point out empty positions, players who aren't here any more, late arrivals placed too early, injuries, and anyone who doesn't get on.
- **Off each quarter**, **Substitutions** and **Minutes** all follow the plan on the pitch. Tick each sub as it happens.

## 4. Sharing

- **Pitch as a picture** (in the share menu, top right) makes a picture of the pitch exactly as it shows on screen for the period you're looking at. It includes the opposition, date, team size and match length at the top, and every box with any subs or position changes underneath the player's name. The bench is included; the 🪄 and ↶ ↷ buttons are left out. Share it straight to WhatsApp. It's view-only, so it never changes anyone else's plan.
  - On **Q1**, each box lists every later change, such as "Q3 Fionn Ryan". That one picture explains the whole plan.
  - Pick another period above the pitch first to picture the team at that point, which is handy at half-time.
- The **share button** (top right of the page) also has:
  - **Send to another phone:** a link to your plan. When another mentor opens it they choose **Save to Plans**, which keeps their own plan, or **Replace my current plan**, which auto-saves theirs first so ↶ brings it back. Players are matched by name. Anyone in the plan who's missing from their panel is added, and nobody is removed.

## 5. Saved plans

- **📁 Plans** (Plan tab) keeps several versions of your plan for a match, for example "Plan A – strong start" and "Plan B – defensive".
  - **Save as new** stores what's on the pitch now, under a name.
  - **Save changes** updates the plan you have open.
  - **Open** switches to a saved plan. **↶** takes you back.
- Your current plan is **auto-saved** before you press 🪄, open another plan, replace it with one from a link, or start a new match. The last 5 auto-saves are kept, so you can always get back to what you were working on.
- Saved plans include who's here, the B and 🔒 marks, the formation and the line-ups. Your panel is shared by all of them.

## Good to know

- Data, including saved plans, stays in the browser on each phone. Nothing syncs between mentors by itself. Add the page to your home screen. On iPhone, Safari can clear data for sites you haven't used in 7 days unless they're on the home screen.
- A link contains the players' names (compressed, not encrypted). Only send it to people who should see the team.
