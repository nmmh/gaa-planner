# GAA Match Planner – Help

**Remember: no plan survives first contact**
Use this tool to trial, visualize and share plans with fellow coaches. 

It is no substitute for reacting to the game that actually plays out. 
++Trust it alone and it might let you down!**

Use it to trial offensive and defensive selections and increase your confidence around what you are trying to achieve in a game. 

A planner for underage hurling and gaelic football mentors. You build your panel once, tick who's here on match day, get a fair starting selection with one tap, and then adjust it by hand.

Everything is saved on your own phone. Nothing is uploaded unless you send a link or a picture.

## The process

1. **Panel tab:** define your panel. For each player choose where they usually play and whether they're a left- or right-sided player.
2. **Here today tab:** set up the match. Tick who's here, set the half length and change times, and mark subs (**B**) and players who stay in one position (**🔒**).
3. **Plan tab:** press the **🪄 wand** for a starting selection across the whole match.
4. **Tweak by hand:** swap players to get the team you want. Use **↶ undo** and **↷ redo** if you change your mind.
5. **Share:** send the pitch as a picture, or send the whole setup to another mentor's phone.

---

## 1. Panel

- The list shows your whole panel. Tap **✎** to change a player's name, positions or side, or to remove them.
- **+ Add player(s)** opens three ways to add players:
  - Type one player's name and tap their positions (GK, FB, HB, MID, HF, FF) and side.
  - **Import a ClubZap RSVP report (.csv).** Anyone not already on the panel is added. Nobody is removed.
  - Paste a list, one player per line, such as `Seán Murphy, HF MID, L`.
- Players marked **no position** still play, but the wand can't place them well. Set their positions first.

## 2. Here today

- **Opposition and date** appear on the plan and on the picture.
- **Half length** and **change times**: tap **Quarters** for changes at each quarter. You can also add your own times.
- **Subs allowed** is **No limit** by default. Choose **Set a limit** if your competition caps substitutions.
- **Who's here:** tick players as they arrive. **Load ClubZap RSVP report** the night before ticks everyone who said they're attending and marks the rest "no reply". Adjust it on the day.
- For each player who's here:
  - **B**: a sub. They start on the bench.
  - **🔒**: stays in one position for the whole match. When they come back on, they go back to the same spot.
  - **⏱**: arrives late, or went off injured at a given minute.
- **Start a new match** clears the ticks, marks and plan. It keeps your panel and settings.

## 3. Plan

- **Team size and formation** are at the top. Formation is written GK-FB-HB-MID-HF-FF, for example `1-2-3-2-3-2`.
- **Q1 / Q2 / Q3 / Q4** show who's where in each quarter. The bench for that quarter is at the bottom of the pitch.
- **🪄 wand** (top left of the pitch) fills in the whole match automatically:
  - It evens out minutes.
  - It keeps 🔒 players in their positions.
  - It starts **B** players on the bench.
  - It works around late arrivals and injuries.
  - Press it again for a different selection. ↶ takes you back.
  - Nothing moves automatically until you press the wand.
- **Swapping players:** tap a player, then another, or press and hold one and drag it.
  - Two players on the pitch swap positions.
  - A bench player swaps in for a pitch player.
  - A swap applies from the quarter you're looking at to the end of the match.
- **↶ / ↷** (top right of the pitch) undo and redo your last actions. They're greyed out when there's nothing to undo or redo. The history lasts until you close the page.
- **Warnings** (yellow) point out empty positions, players who aren't here any more, late arrivals placed too early, injuries, and anyone who doesn't get on.
- **Off each quarter**, **Substitutions** and **Minutes** all follow the plan on the pitch. Tick each sub as it happens.

## 4. Sharing

- **📷 Image** (Plan tab) makes a picture of this quarter or all four quarters, with the opposition and date. Share it straight to WhatsApp. It's view-only, so it never changes anyone else's plan.
- The **share button** (top right of the page) also has:
  - **Send to another phone:** a link that loads your whole setup (panel, attendance and plan) on another phone. Opening it replaces what's on that phone, after asking.
  - **Plan as WhatsApp text**
  - **Panel list**
  - **Load from a link**

## Good to know

- Data stays in the browser on each phone. Add the page to your home screen. On iPhone, Safari can clear data for sites you haven't used in 7 days unless they're on the home screen.
- A link contains the players' names (compressed, not encrypted). Only send it to people who should see the team.
